##### Column: Rec.

Total nucleotide length of the putative recombinant regions of the sequence. A hidden Markov model finds the regions
where the sequence diverges strongly from its parent on the reference tree. A sequence gets no call when its regions
cover most of the sequence, or when the rest of the sequence outside the regions has too many private mutations.

The column is shown only for datasets that enable recombination detection.

Hover over a cell for region details and percentages.
